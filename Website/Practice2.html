A Grateful Universe

"I thought by eliminating half of life, the other half would thrive. But you've shown me... that's impossible. As long as there are those that remember what was, there will always be those that are unable to accept what could be. They will resist. I'm not grateful. You see, I now know what I must do. I will shred this universe down to its last atom and then, with the stones you've collected for me, create a new one. It is teemed with life that knows not what it has lost, but only what it has been given. A grateful universe."
